# Product Context

*Slower-moving than activeContext.md — read when you need the "why," not every session.*

## Problems this app exists to solve
- Generic AI chat requires the user to already know how to ask well; most people don't know
  how to structure a self-taught curriculum on their own.
- People struggle to tell credible sources from noise/sponsored content when learning on
  their own from the open internet.
- Passive consumption (reading/watching) is mistaken for understanding; without being
  tested, gaps stay invisible.
- Producing work without feedback lets errors and bad habits compound unnoticed.
- Deep focus on one topic narrows thinking; deliberate cross-domain exposure is rare because
  nobody schedules it for themselves.
- Most self-directed learning attempts (courses, plans) are abandoned — the tooling around
  them doesn't help people pick back up after a gap.

Full reasoning and the principle each maps to: `docs/00-PRINCIPLES.md`.

## How it should feel to use
- Starting a new topic should feel like being interviewed by someone who actually wants to
  understand where you are, not filling out a form.
- Session-to-session, the app should feel like it remembers — no re-explaining context.
- Feedback (Tutor gap notes, Editor reviews) should feel specific and a little
  uncomfortable, never like empty encouragement.
- Coming back after a break should feel like picking up a conversation, not starting over.
- On a mobile phone, it should feel like an intentional, calm, distraction-free pocket reading
  room — installable via PWA with bottom navigation, responsive tap targets, and proactive
  serendipitous insights.

## Web-Hosted & Multi-User Dimension (Phases 12–16)
- Any learner can sign up with their email and password to receive their own completely
  isolated private university.
- AI costs are sustainable and transparent: learners can bring their own API keys (BYOK)
  encrypted at rest, or take advantage of an initial free trial quota provided by the host.
- Absolute data sovereignty: learners have full agency (P7) to export their entire curriculum,
  interviews, and review history to Markdown/JSON, or delete their account permanently.

## Relationship to the source material
The five roles (Advisor, Librarian, Tutor, Editor, Roommate) map directly to five roles
described in the source video, each illustrated there with its own real-world analogy
(a personalized academic advisor, a research librarian curating trustworthy sources, a
one-on-one tutor, an editor sharpening someone's work, and a roommate whose different
background broadens your thinking). The product exists to make those five roles into real,
persistent software features rather than something the user has to remember to ask an AI
chat for each time.
